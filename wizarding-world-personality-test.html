Before You Arrive

A Short Test of Character

Before your first Sunday evening, we ask that you complete this short questionnaire. It will take less than two minutes. There are no wrong answers — only honest ones.

What you discover about yourself may surprise you. It will certainly be relevant on the night.

Question 1 of 10
Question 1 of 10
You find a wallet on the ground with $200 cash inside and no ID. What do you do?
Question 2 of 10
A fire breaks out nearby. What is your first instinct?
Question 3 of 10
Which quality do you value most in a close friend?
Question 4 of 10
You have a free Saturday with no obligations whatsoever. What do you do?
Question 5 of 10
How would you most want to be remembered?
Question 6 of 10
Your group is about to make a decision you believe is wrong. What do you do?
Question 7 of 10
You are offered a shortcut that breaks no rules — but it feels slightly off. Do you take it?
Question 8 of 10
What kind of problem solver are you?
Question 9 of 10
Which class would you most want to take at Hogwarts?
Question 10 of 10
One last question. When you walk into a room full of strangers, what do people tend to notice about you first?
